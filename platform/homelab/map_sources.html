Map sources and credits

The same sources are used on the web and in Desktop. Detailed online basemaps and place search require Internet access; a coarse NOAA terrain backup is bundled locally. Your local projects and processing remain independent of these services.

3D display and terrain

CesiumJS is distributed under Apache 2.0. The default map does not use Cesium ion.

Re:Earth Terrain supplies native Cesium quantized meshes, normally through MosaicEarth’s terrain-only endpoint and cache, from Mapterhorn elevation data, converted to WGS84 ellipsoidal heights using EGM2008 (NGA). Detail varies by location. Re:Earth is an open, best-effort service without an SLA or an API key; access may be rate-limited. No Cesium ion subscription is used.

Data attribution: Re:Earth Terrain · Mapterhorn (CC BY 4.0 and the regional source terms listed in Mapterhorn's full attribution catalogue) · EGM2008 (NGA, public domain). Provider metadata may also credit Protomaps and © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); MosaicEarth does not request the optional water mask.

Polar coverage: Re:Earth's upstream elevation source uses Web Mercator and does not provide reliable ice-surface terrain beyond approximately 85° latitude. MosaicEarth therefore blends in NOAA NCEI ETOPO 2022 between 84° and 85° north/south, with ETOPO alone beyond 85°. These bundled, open CC0-1.0 display grids use the 60 arc-second ice surface plus its EGM2008 geoid, resampled to 1 arc-minute latitude and 0.25° longitude (about 1.9 km north/south, at most 2.9 km east/west). They show the ice surface and sea surface, not seafloor bathymetry. They are coarse contextual terrain, not local survey data.

Polar data citation: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (2022), ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model, 60 arc-second derivative, accessed 9 November 2026. Licence, limitations and metadata. NOAA dedicates the data worldwide under CC0-1.0. Not for navigation.

Availability and caching: the stable terrain.mosaicearth.app endpoint uses Cloudflare CDN and a bounded persistent cache on the MosaicEarth server. Only requested public terrain is cached; this is not a permanent copy of the planet. If the gateway fails, the viewer tries Re:Earth directly. If detailed terrain is unavailable, it shows a visible notice and uses a bundled NOAA ETOPO 2022 global grid at 0.25° spacing (about 28 km at the equator), together with the polar grids above. This low-resolution fallback is for geographic context. It does not provide detailed terrain or offline satellite imagery.

Background terrain is for visualization, not a surveyed reference or a correction of the vertical datum of your files. Project DEMs and DSMs retain their own coordinates, vertical metadata and access controls. Public base-map caching never publishes private project data.

Requests reveal the requested map tiles and connection IP to the service receiving them: normally MosaicEarth/Cloudflare, or Re:Earth during direct fallback. No account password, activation grant or project file is sent with base-terrain requests.

The open-source legacy Mapzen adapter remains available for compatibility and comparison. Its underlying sources and licences are listed below; it is no longer the automatic fallback.

Satellite and aerial imagery

The detailed global background uses Esri World Imagery through ArcGIS Location Platform. Powered by Esri. The viewer displays the data suppliers credited by the service. Resolution, capture date and positional accuracy vary by location; worldwide centimetre-level detail is not guaranteed. This is a contextual basemap, not survey evidence.

Esri imagery is a licensed online service, separate from the open-source MosaicEarth application. It is not bundled with the source code or installers and is not relicensed under the AGPL. Access is subject to the ArcGIS Location Platform terms and applicable data restrictions. Keep the displayed attribution. MosaicEarth does not offer bulk downloads, offline imagery packages or a shared Esri tile cache.

A short-lived, individual map session is issued by the MosaicEarth server without requiring a MosaicEarth login. Tiles then load directly from Esri. Esri receives the requested geographic tiles, the connection IP and ordinary request metadata; account passwords, activation grants and project files are not sent. The broker records a random session identifier and a daily pseudonymous connection identifier to enforce service limits; it does not record the viewed coordinates. If the service allowance is exhausted or detailed imagery fails, the viewer shows a notice and uses the open background below. Source deployments can select open imagery only or configure their own session broker.

Open global fallback: EOxCloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC BY 4.0. This is the 2016 mosaic, not current high-resolution aerial imagery. Enlarging it does not add detail. Service and licence information.

At closer zooms in mainland Spain, the Balearic and Canary Islands: PNOA · IGN 2022–2025, scne.es, CC BY 4.0, served by IGN. Capture year and resolution depend on the location. The public-domain Natural Earth Spain boundary is used to select this service; it is not a survey boundary. Tiles around the national boundary can contain the service's Sentinel overview. If this service fails, the global provider is used, with the open 2016 mosaic as the last fallback.

Street map and place search

© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The Topographic button shows the standard OSM street map draped on 3D terrain; it is not a contour-map layer. Report a map issue.

Place search uses Photon and OSM data. Only submitted searches are sent; typing does not make autocomplete requests. Coordinates can be entered without a place-search service. Desktop uses the public MosaicEarth search endpoint without sending account credentials.

Public tile services operate on a best-effort basis, with attribution and reasonable-use conditions. No bulk/offline map downloads are offered. Browser and CDN caches follow the response HTTP headers; retention and detailed service availability are not guaranteed. These map licences do not change the ownership or licence of your uploaded data.
