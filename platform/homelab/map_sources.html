Map sources and credits

The same sources are used on the web and in Desktop. Basemaps and place search require Internet access. Your local projects and processing remain independent of these services.

3D display and terrain

CesiumJS is distributed under Apache 2.0. The default map does not use Cesium ion.

Mapzen Terrain Tiles are hosted by the AWS Open Data program. MosaicEarth resamples Terrarium elevation pixels into the 3D globe. Detail varies by region; elevation beyond Web Mercator coverage (85.05° north/south) extends the edge value. This background terrain is for visualization, not a surveyed reference or a correction of the vertical datum of your files.

Satellite and aerial imagery

Global background: EOxCloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC BY 4.0. This is the 2016 mosaic, not current high-resolution aerial imagery. Enlarging it does not add detail. Service and licence information.

At closer zooms in mainland Spain, the Balearic and Canary Islands: PNOA · IGN 2022–2025, scne.es, CC BY 4.0, served by IGN. Capture year and resolution depend on the location. The public-domain Natural Earth Spain boundary is used to select this service; it is not a survey boundary. Tiles around the national boundary can contain the service's Sentinel overview. If this service fails, the global 2016 mosaic is used.

Street map and place search

© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The Topographic button shows the standard OSM street map draped on 3D terrain; it is not a contour-map layer. Report a map issue.

Place search uses Photon and OSM data. Only submitted searches are sent; typing does not make autocomplete requests. Coordinates can be entered without a place-search service. Desktop uses the public MosaicEarth search endpoint without sending account credentials.

Public tile services operate on a best-effort basis, with attribution and reasonable-use conditions. No bulk/offline map downloads are offered. Existing browser cache follows provider HTTP headers. These map licences do not change the ownership or licence of your uploaded data.
